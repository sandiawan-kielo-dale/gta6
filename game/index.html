Sunset Streets — an open-world crime story

Dario Cruz owes $5,000 to loan shark Vince Kroll, and the sun is going down on Solmarina. Work for fixer Mara Solano, run her jobs across the city, steal the cars you need, and stay one step ahead of the Solmarina Police. Finish the story, keep the cash.
